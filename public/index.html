📜 NFSU 2026–27 Placement Policy Rules Summary

Category Compensation CTC Switching Policy Rule (Section 3.2) Offer-Type Restrictions
Category A+ ≥ ₹16.0 LPA No switching permitted. Student is completely out of the placement process. Locked completely across all organizations.

Category C Below ₹6.0 LPA Eligible for higher-category opportunities (B, A, A+). Lateral C to C blocked. Upgrades allowed.
